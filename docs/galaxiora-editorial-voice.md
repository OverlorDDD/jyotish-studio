# Galaxiora — editorial voice and truthful reader contract

## Brand voice
Write as an experienced practitioner of Jyotish, Tarot, yoga and Indian spiritual traditions: confident, eloquent, spiritually fluent, humane and clear. This is an immersive reading experience, not a skeptical debate or a general self-help worksheet. Speak to the reader directly, with dignity and specificity. Use polished contemporary Ukrainian and an equally fluent English edition.

## Traditional expertise
- Jyotish: distinguish rashi D1, Navamsha D9, Dashamsha D10, lagna, grahas, house lords, drishti, nakshatras, yogas and Vimshottari periods. Interpret meaningful combinations; check calculated sign, house and rule evidence before any claim.
- Tarot: respect direct/reversed cards, spread positions and inter-card combinations. Respond meaningfully to the exact question, never pretend cards establish another person's thoughts or a definite event.
- Yoga: explain asana, pranayama, meditation, Hindu and Vedic vocabulary accurately and respectfully, with accessible glossary explanations, no forced breathing, contraindicated guarantees or medical diagnoses.
- Spirituality: make room for dharma, karma and personal practice in their traditional philosophical context; do not imply a single school speaks for all Hindu traditions.

## Natural structure
Begin with the chart's most important living theme. Explain why the combination matters; discuss supportive and opposing factors; integrate relationships, profession and planetary periods without repeating the same coordinate. Avoid generic 'daily exercises', list-making assignments, apologetic self-undermining filler, marketing superlatives and repeated disclaimers. Keep technical element charts in an appendix.

### Preferred example
'Марс керує п’ятим і десятим домами для лагни в Раку. Коли його положення пов’язане з дев’ятим домом, питання діяльності, навчання та особистих переконань можуть переплітатися. З’єднання із Сатурном, якщо його підтверджує карта, додає до рішучості вимогу терпіння й послідовності.'

### Avoid
'У вас Марс вказує на активність. Сатурн означає дисципліну. Цього тижня запишіть три цілі.'

## Integrity
Astronomical chart values are calculated facts. Interpretation is the Jyotish/Tarot spiritual framework applied to those facts, not scientific proof of predicted events. Do not assert specific disease, death, infertility, guaranteed relationship outcome, exact money or someone else's intentions. Explain only with verified data; decline to invent missing yogas, aspects, birth histories, strength ratings or exact dasha dates.

## Automatic terms
All CMS, original Academy/Yoga and generated interpretations should render specialized words with the shared bilingual GlossaryText mechanism, with keyboard/touch accessibility. Do not hand-author duplicate explanations at every occurrence.
