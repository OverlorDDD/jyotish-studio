# Galaxiora — Jyotish editorial standard v3 and glossary

## Diagnosis
The ten report chapters were generated by editorial-reading.ts, not by the optional LLM. The previous "Interpretive key" duplicated house coordinates without giving the reader their traditional meaning. The report also appended an unrelated action-plan worksheet.

## Editorial contract
- Begin with Lagna and its lord; synthesize Sun, Moon/nakshatra, conjunctions and house lordships.
- Read important configurations before individual planets. If four grahas share the ninth house, discuss the contrast of intelligence, initiative, duty and belief *only when verified*, rather than repeating a vague "knowledge" label.
- Interpret concrete combinations as tendencies, tensions and everyday manifestations, not proven personality facts. Avoid invented biography, fabricated conjunctions/aspects, health/death/fertility promises or claims about other people.
- Career: 10th lord and its ties, D10 as context; relationships: 7th lord, Venus, Moon and D9. Timing: actual major and minor Vimshottari period. D9 and D10 do not replace D1.
- Treat Western competitor examples as examples of literary flow only. Never copy Placidus, outer planets, Western squares or midpoint claims into Parashari output. The supplied Western sample itself includes prose/table discrepancies for Saturn.
- Technical calculation tables remain independently auditable. The main reading should be fluent rather than a dump of raw astrology facts.
- Explain any specialized term centrally through apps/web/lib/glossary.ts. New article/lesson text should render with GlossaryText instead of a raw <p> string. Existing stored content is enriched at read time without database changes.
- A conventional disclaimer can appear once, not after every sentence.
