# Astrological Synthesis Engine (ASE) v1 — research and release design
Galaxiora · 2026-10-31

## Why
Users have Western tropical Sun signs, Vedic sidereal Moon signs and verified
Lagna. One sky does not mean interchangeable interpretations. We need
independent, traceable readings and a *genuine*, not forced, common theme.
This is professional traditional interpretation, with astronomy separated
from editorial mapping and from guarantees of outcomes.

## Core v1 contract (implemented in this feature branch)

**Astronomy / reference time**
- Private authenticated, verified primary natal chart. Birth UTC generated by
  existing verified chart engine, never accepted as user query parameters.
- Tropical native Swiss: natal Sun + Moon from exact birth UTC, transiting
  Sun Moon Mercury Venus Mars Jupiter Saturn at local noon.
- Western contact aspects: 0, 60, 90, 120, 180 degrees, tight longitude
  circular-distance math; <=3° orbs (fast), <=4° (Jupiter, Saturn).
  No fabricated exact ingress, aspect duration or progression timings.
- Vedic: existing Swiss/Lahiri JHora-8-compatible sidereal gochara from
  natal Moon and separately from birth-time Lagna, with verified returned
  SWIEPH backend and same UTC sample. Uses only already-certified chart data.
  Actual Vimshottari lords may be shown as timing context; NOT treated as
  precise personal destiny scores in v1.

**Independent evidence**
Each signal records a deterministic ID, tradition, planet, source method,
observation UTC, current longitude, natal longitude/aspect/orb (Western) or
whole-sign house/natal anchor (Vedic).

**Comparison**
The two sets of signals are independently assigned editorial topic labels.
A "shared" topic exists only when both independently include it.
Otherwise report "different emphases"; if no close western natal aspect
report "limited evidence". Never hallucinate a consensus, unsupported
planet, numeric prediction probability or event certainty.
All advice is human-authored bilingual, finite, applicable without knowing
a user's occupation, and tied to a stated topic. Same sky may produce same
advice, without fake daily rotation.

**Privacy / API**
GET /api/v1/personal/horoscope/synthesis?days=1 or 7; owner session checked
before accessing stored chart. Cache-Control private,no-store. No personal
birth dates, addresses, emails or coordinates in URL. No DB writes, LLM or
stored inference artifacts. Rejected if any source longitude, timezone,
verification proof or celestial backend provenance is missing.

**UI**
Visible in "Мій персональний"/"My personal reading" as a third selector
"Синтез карти"/"Chart synthesis". Distinct from public twelve-sign mode,
and from existing Western/Vedic personal reading content. Day and week
results have independently expandable detailed astronomy for each method.
Human-readable UA and EN explanations.

## Roadmap

ASE-1 (this PR): strict evidence comparator + private day/week API, 2-language
UI, complete release gates and regression testing.

ASE-2 (future): exact Western natal house cusps and full seven-planet natal
contacts, applying/separating, date-of-exact-aspect solver and event duration;
verified Jyotish graha drishti, dasha weighting, Ashtakavarga, Tara Bala;
additional classical source provenance and 20+ natal reference benchmarks.
Unknown birth time suppresses Lagna/houses, not guesses.

ASE-3 (future): constrained AI phrasing layer allowed to summarize only
validated evidence; explain nuanced tension/contradictions without forcing
harmony; season timelines and editorial approvals. No invented positions.

## Limitations of ASE v1
This is a deliberately narrow traditional transit comparison, not a full
Western natal interpretation and not a fully certified graha drishti/dasha
forecast. Accurate ephemeris coordinates do not empirically establish
future events; the synthesis faithfully communicates interpretive themes.
Full JQA-3 Lagna/Moon arcsecond parity is a separate unresolved gate.
