# GalaxiCom C5 — Growth, reputation and future PREMIUM (2026-10-26)

## Scope / acceptance
C5 closes the planned forum development sequence C0–C5, but is not a production launch authorization. Owner manual acceptance (especially C2–C5), C4 operations, and explicit deployment approval remain.

### Corrected writing lengths (owner clarification)
The request was about removing MINIMUM character requirements; the original MAXIMUM values are restored.
- New topic title: 1–160 chars after trim.
- Topic body: 1–8,000 chars after trim.
- Reply: 1–5,000 chars after trim.
- Topic and reply editing use the same limits.
- A whitespace-only message remains invalid (no meaningful text). Server still rejects HTML/script, limits external links, request body to 2 MiB and has per-account rate quotas.
- Full-width resizable editor remains. Old C2 test expectations for 2,000/200,000 are revised rather than weakening the validation test.

### Growth & discovery
- Public `GET /api/v1/com/growth/featured`: top six *currently published* topics among the last 30 days, sorted by visible reply activity and recency. Not an editorial recommendation; no hidden/deleted content, unverified testimonials, private questions or paid boost.
- Public `GET /api/v1/com/growth/members/:id`: reputation derived only from helpful votes by OTHER accounts on public published replies. Four simple levels: NEW (0–2), ACTIVE (3–9), HELPFUL (10–24), GUIDE (25+). Not an EXPERT credential. No role changes based on reputation.
- Private bookmarks: `GET /growth/bookmarks`, `GET /growth/bookmarks/:id`, `PUT /growth/bookmarks/:id`, `DELETE /growth/bookmarks/:id`. Unique per user, hidden/deleted discussions excluded from lists, idempotent writes, same-origin on writes and SERIALIZABLE cap enforcement. FREE maximum 20; active PREMIUM maximum 200. No access to another member's bookmarks.
- Profile `PATCH /growth/profile`: free public bio up to 80 characters and default accent. Verified active PREMIUM supports up to 280 and gold/violet/teal accents, effective immediately. If subscription expires, public accent resets and public bio is truncated to 80. Badge opt-in remains separately available. Premium does NOT unlock moderator authority, EXPERT status, voting influence, or exclusive posting rooms.
- User-facing bilingual growth panel for trending topics, bookmarks and profile settings; public member page shows the computed reputation and custom (active PREMIUM only) accent. All labels UA/EN.
- No new payment gateway; existing tier activation is privileged/system-controlled only. No simulated checkout or unverified paid purchases.
